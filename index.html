About

Engineer first, designer by craft

I build conversational AI end to end: dialogue strategy, intent taxonomies, conversation flows, prompt engineering, RAG grounding, and the production plumbing that keeps assistants reliable — evaluation, guardrails, observability, and escalation design.

At Eli Lilly I designed multi-turn conversational workflows for CMC portfolio knowledge retrieval. At Janssen (J&J) I built Copilot Studio–based healthcare assistants with human-in-the-loop escalation. At Bank of America I modernized Erica's dialogue capabilities with voice support. I care about conversations that respect the user's time: clear intent handling, honest fallbacks, and designs grounded in how people actually talk.
